Project Summary

This Tableau project brings together geographical, temporal, and comparative views of global fuel prices and taxation. The dashboard supports exploration of regional price volatility, country-level diesel and petrol prices, income-group differences, subsidy categories, tax percentages, and the relationship between Brent crude and fuel prices. Interactive filters help users focus on specific subsets of the data and communicate findings through the published dashboard and guided story.
